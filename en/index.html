How We Work

The format fits your moment — not the other way around

Three ways to start. All of them begin with the same question: what do you need to solve right now?

Single Session

"Need a perspective right now?"

A structured conversation for diagnosis and direction. Ideal for unlocking a decision, testing a hypothesis, or thinking out loud with someone who's seen this movie before.

Monthly Retainer

"Want a constant strategic partner?"

Recurring presence in your operation: periodic meetings, review of indicators, and availability for decisions that can't wait until next month.

2–3 Month Project

"Have a project with defined scope?"

A concrete deliverable — strategic plan, commercial structuring, expansion plan — with a defined beginning, middle, and end.

No model is a closed box. Many clients combine formats over time — hybrid arrangements are welcome. Start wherever makes sense; we'll adjust the rest in conversation.
