Sport & Wellness · Scale-up · Brazil Ongoing engagement — Advisory Board since 2022

How to scale a commercial operation that depended on the founder

Kokua Sport Care & Wellness was growing over 30% a year, but with a commercial operation concentrated in the founder and no structure to scale.

The core findings of the diagnosis

>80%
What we foundof B2B deals depended exclusively on the founder — a critical structural risk for scaling.
RecommendationHire a senior B2B sales executive and create a two-level approval policy.
Expected impactThe founder is freed up for strategic decisions.
5–8×
What we foundaverage ticket in B2B vs. B2C — yet the B2B channel got less than half of the commercial focus.
RecommendationRebalance the channel mix to 70% B2B over the 2026–2028 projection period.
Expected impactRevenue growth without a proportional increase in cost.
>35%
What we foundof annual revenue concentrated in a single quarter — a fragile seasonal base for any expansion.
RecommendationPhased expansion roadmap: specialized chains → gyms → mass retail.
Expected impactLower volatility and a recurring revenue base.

Preparing a complex strategic partnership: from briefing to the negotiating table

A company needed to make a strategic partnership possible with a highly specialized external partner. The challenge: turn a well-understood internal vision into an executive presentation the partner could evaluate, understand and want to be part of.

The process — from briefing to negotiation

1. Strategic Briefing
  • Immersion in the project and context
  • Mapping the target partner
  • Goals of the conversation
  • Clarity on what needs to be said
2. Narrative Structure
  • Problem → solution → gain logic
  • Value opportunities for the partner
  • Flow and hierarchy
  • Validation before the visual work
3. Executive Deck
  • 10–15 slides built around the partner
  • Executive language and clean visuals
  • Delivered editable and as PDF
  • Ready for the meeting and for sending
4. Iterative Refinement
  • 3 review cycles with the client
  • Anticipating the partner's questions
  • Full walk-through of the material
  • Final pre-meeting alignment

Result: meeting with the strategic partner secured; the client led the negotiation with clarity and confidence.

Frequently Asked Questions

What people usually ask before talking

What does an independent strategy consultant do?

An independent strategy consultant helps a company decide where to go, prioritize what matters and turn that into an executable plan, without being tied to a large firm. Diogo Pires de Oliveira works directly with the client and stays involved until the plan becomes action.

How is an independent consultant different from a large consulting firm?

In large firms there is often a partner who sells and a junior team that delivers. With Conexão.fr you talk directly to the person who thinks and executes, with no overhead and no 90-day adaptation period before value starts to show.

What kind of company and moment is this work right for?

SMEs, scale-ups and founders who have a vision but can't get it off the page; whose sales depend on a few people; who need to build structure before scaling; or who want to grow between Brazil and France without improvising.

How does the first contact work?

With a 30-minute conversation, no commitment, booked directly on the site. You don't need to have everything figured out beforehand: the conversation is there to understand where you are, where you want to go and which format makes the most sense.

What working formats are available?

A single 90-minute session with a written summary; a monthly retainer with four 60-minute sessions and availability in between; or a 2 to 3 month project with defined scope and deliverables. Hybrid models and other forms of collaboration are welcome.

How much does independent strategy consulting cost?

The investment depends on the format and scope. In the 30-minute conversation I understand your challenge and recommend the most suitable format, with a clear proposal before any commitment.

Do you work with companies between Brazil and France?

Yes. Diogo Pires de Oliveira has more than 20 years of experience between the two countries and works in Portuguese, French and English. Support includes market validation, entry strategy, business model adaptation and identifying local partners.
